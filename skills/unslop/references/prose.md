# Prose

Load the **writing-style** skill. It holds the rules this pass applies; this file holds what is specific to rewriting a document that already exists.

Work top-down through writing-style's sections, starting with the reader and the doc's job. Fix structure before wording, since structure costs the reader more.

Two of its rules change for a rewrite:

- **One job per doc.** When a doc serves several readers or jobs, flag the split in the reply. Don't split it unasked.
- **No meta commentary.** A real maintenance rule found in meta commentary belongs in the repo's agent instructions or a script; cut it from the doc and flag it in the reply.

## Don't overcorrect

- Keep every fact, number, link, command, condition, and caveat about the subject that the reader needs.
- Keep the connectives ("because", "but", "unless", "which means"). They carry how the facts relate, and the verifier checks facts, not links.
- Keep domain terms the reader's codebase or team uses.
- Keep the author's voice where it is plain already. Change what's broken, not what's merely different from how you would write it.
